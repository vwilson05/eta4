A letter from the founder

Why we paused, and why we are coming back stronger than ever

Victor Wilson, Founder and Executive Director. September 2026. About 6 minutes.

When we wrapped up our 2019 summer program in Hue, we had no idea it would be our last for eight years.

What started as eta4 in 2009, a simple dream to teach English through Academics, Athletics and Arts, had grown beyond anything I imagined. We had taught over 14,000 students across five cities in Vietnam and Taiwan. We had trained hundreds of volunteers. We had built lasting partnerships with local schools and communities. The program was thriving.

And then life happened.

The perfect storm

In early 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic shut down international travel and gatherings worldwide. Suddenly, bringing international volunteers to Vietnamese classrooms became impossible. Our Summer 2020 program was cancelled. Then 2021. Then 2022.

But if I am being completely honest, COVID was not the only reason we paused.

During those same years, my wife and I welcomed our first child into the world. Becoming a parent changed everything: my priorities, my time, my energy. Spending summers away from my growing family felt impossible. The logistics of coordinating programs from halfway around the world while caring for a newborn became overwhelming.

Life was pulling me in a different direction, and I had to listen.

The guilt of stepping away

Stepping away from eta4 was one of the hardest decisions I have ever made. Every summer that passed felt like letting down the students in Hue who were waiting for our program. Letting down the local volunteers who had built their teaching skills with us. Letting down the international volunteers who dreamed of making a difference.

But I also knew that trying to run the program at half capacity, burned out, distracted, and unable to give it my full attention, would do more harm than good. Our students and volunteers deserved better than that.

What we learned during the pause

The unexpected gift of these past six years has been perspective. Stepping back let me reflect on what made eta4 special, and what we could do better.

We realized the power of local leadership. Our Vietnam program coordinator, herself a former student from our 2013 program, kept the spirit of eta4 alive in Hue even while our international programs were on hold. She continued training local volunteers, maintaining relationships with schools, and proving that sustainable impact comes from empowering local teachers, not just flying in for five weeks each summer.

We learned the importance of rest and renewal. Burnout is real, especially in nonprofit work. Taking time to recharge, to be present with my family, to plan thoughtfully rather than react constantly, has made me a better leader.

We understood that pausing is not quitting. Sometimes the most loving thing you can do for something you care about is to give it, and yourself, the space to grow.

Why Summer 2027?

So why now? Because we are ready. Really ready.

My daughter is now old enough that summer programs feel manageable again. The world has reopened. And most importantly, the fire is back. I wake up excited about eta4 in a way I have not felt in years.

But we are not just picking up where we left off. We are coming back smarter, more focused, and more committed to sustainable impact.

What is different this time

1. Local volunteers are at the center. Our 2027 program will be a true partnership between international and Vietnamese volunteers. We are investing heavily in training local teachers who can continue the work year-round, not just during our five-week summer program.

2. Smaller, more intentional. We are starting with just Hue in 2027: one location, 50 volunteers, and a laser focus on quality over quantity. Trying to run programs in five cities at once spread us too thin. Deep impact in one community beats shallow presence in many.

3. Better support for volunteers. We have redesigned volunteer training, mentorship and support based on feedback from our veterans. Teaching abroad is challenging, and we want to set everyone up to succeed.

4. Radical transparency. We are committing to share everything: our financials, our challenges, our metrics, our failures and our successes. You deserve to know exactly how your support makes a difference.

An invitation

If you are reading this, you are part of the eta4 story. Maybe you volunteered with us in 2011. Maybe you donated to support a classroom. Maybe you are hearing about us for the first time today.

Wherever you are in the journey, I want to invite you to be part of our return.

We need volunteers for Summer 2027 in Hue. If you have six weeks, a passion for education, and the flexibility to spend your summer in Vietnam, we want you on our team.

We need donors to rebuild our program infrastructure, buy new teaching materials, and support local volunteer training. Every dollar goes directly toward lasting impact.

We need cheerleaders to spread the word, follow our journey, and remind us why this work matters on the hard days.

The best is yet to come

Looking back at 14,250 students, I am filled with gratitude for what eta4 has accomplished. I am even more excited about what is ahead.

The pandemic taught us that education is resilient. Communities are resilient. And with the right support, students everywhere can thrive, no matter what the world throws at them.

We paused because we had to. We are returning because we are ready to do this work better than ever before.

Let's make Summer 2027 the beginning of eta4's next chapter, together.
