Your summer

Week zero to week five.

Week 0

Orientation

Arrive in Hue, meet your co-teacher, practice lessons on each other, learn the city, eat everything.

Weeks 1 to 2

Foundations

Level tests, classroom routines, confidence games. Your students start speaking on day one.

Weeks 3 to 4

Full teaching

Mornings of academics, afternoons of sport and art. You lead, your local co-teacher runs alongside.

Week 5

Showcase

Students perform for families. You hand your materials and your class to the local team.

After

Alumni

The year-round program in Hue continues without you, which is the point. You stay in the story.

What it costs, honestly

We cover the program. You cover getting there.

Volunteers pay their own flights and personal spending. Most people spend between $1,500 and $2,500 total depending on where they fly from. There is no program fee.

Covered by eta4

Shared housing in Hue for six weeks, training and orientation, all teaching materials, a local co-teacher, airport pickup, weekday lunches at school.

You cover

Round-trip flights, Vietnam e-visa (about $25), travel insurance, dinners and weekends, any travel before or after camp.

Questions

What volunteers ask first.

Anything else: info@eta4.org. A real person answers.

Do I need teaching experience?
No. Orientation week trains you, and you teach with a local co-teacher every day. Enthusiasm, patience and reliability matter more than credentials.
How long is the commitment?
Six weeks in Hue: orientation June 28 to July 2, then camp July 5 to August 6, 2027. Partial summers are not possible because your class depends on you.
Who are the students?
Vietnamese students aged 8 to 18 from public schools in Hue, grouped by age and English level. The camp is free for every family.
Where will I live?
Shared volunteer housing in Hue, a few minutes from the school. Photos and a packing list are in the info pack.
Is it safe?
Hue is a calm, mid-sized city and we have run ten summers there. Orientation covers health, transport and local norms, and staff are on call around the clock.
Can Vietnamese students and teachers volunteer?
Yes, and they are at the center of the 2027 program. See the local volunteer page in Vietnamese: Tình nguyện viên địa phương.
When will I hear back?
Applications open November 2, 2026 and close March 31, 2027. We review in rounds and confirm spots by April 15, 2027.
