Year by year

Where we taught, and how many.

2009

Our beginning

Hue, Vietnam. 350 students, one school, the first volunteers. Cumulative: 350.

2010

Expansion

Hue and Bien Hoa, Vietnam, plus Taitung, Taiwan. 1,100 students. Cumulative: 1,450.

2011

Growing

Hue and Da Lat, Vietnam, and Taitung, Taiwan. 1,300 students. Cumulative: 2,750.

2012

Focus on Hue

Hue only. 1,100 students. Cumulative: 3,850.

2013

Record year

Hue and Duc Linh, Phan Thiet Province. 2,000 students. Cumulative: 5,850.

2014

Continued growth

Hue and Duc Linh. 2,300 students. Cumulative: 8,150.

2015

Peak

Hue and Duc Linh. 2,500 students, our largest summer. Cumulative: 10,650.

2016 to 2018

Sustained

Hue. About 1,000 students each summer. Cumulative: 13,650.

2019

Last before the pause

Hue. 600 students. Cumulative: 14,250.

2020 to 2026

Paused

COVID-19 closed borders and classrooms, then life did the rest. The founder's letter tells it plainly.

2027

The return

Hue. One city, 50 volunteers, local co-teachers in every class. July 5 to August 6, 2027.
