How it works

Learn the language by using it for something you love.

Textbook English does not survive contact with a real conversation. So we build the camp around things kids already want to do, and run all of it in English.

A morning of reading, writing, speaking and listening, grouped by level. An afternoon of football, basketball, dance, music, drama and painting, coached in English. In week five, students perform for their families, and the local volunteers who taught alongside us take over the year-round program.

The four A's

Academics, Athletics, Arts, Abroad.

Academics

Reading, writing, speaking and listening every morning, in small groups matched to each student's level.

Học thuật

Athletics

Football, basketball, relays and dance in the afternoon. Teams mix ages and levels, and every instruction is in English.

Thể thao

Arts

Music, drama, drawing and a rap lesson that is now legend. Kids find a voice in English before they find the grammar for it.

Nghệ thuật

Abroad

International volunteers teach with local university students and teachers. When the summer ends, the local team carries on.

Quốc tế

Ten summers of numbers

From 350 students in one Hue school to 14,250 across five cities.

We started in 2009 with one school and a handful of volunteers. By 2015 we were teaching 2,500 students a summer in Hue and Duc Linh. Then COVID, and life, paused us. The numbers are real, and so is the gap.
