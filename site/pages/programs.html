The camp

Five weeks. Every morning a lesson, every afternoon a reason to use it.

An eta4 camp is free for every family and open to students aged 8 to 18. Here is how it works, and how a family in Hue joins the 2027 camp.

Đọc bằng tiếng Việt

A day at camp

Morning: academics. Afternoon: everything else.

Students arrive at 7:30 and start with a warm-up song in English. Mornings are small-group lessons in reading, writing, speaking and listening, taught by an international volunteer and a local co-teacher together. After lunch, the same students rotate through sport, music, drama and art, all coached in English. Camp ends at 4:30.

Every Friday the whole camp comes together for a challenge: a spelling relay, a drama round, a song. In week five, families come to the showcase.

Who it is for

Public school students in Hue, ages 8 to 18, at every level of English.

The five weeks

Confidence first, then skill, then a stage.

Weeks 1 to 2

Foundations

Level assessment, classroom English, games that make speaking normal. Vocabulary for school, home and sport.

Weeks 3 to 4

Skill building

Reading real texts, writing letters and raps, listening to each other, presenting to the class. Sport and art blocks get more demanding.

Week 5

Showcase

Performances, presentations and competitions for families. Certificates for every student. The local team takes over for the year.

Year-round in Hue

Five weeks is a spark. Local volunteers keep it lit.

Since 2013 our former students and local teachers have run English activities in Hue between summers. The 2027 camp is built around them: every class has a Vietnamese co-teacher who continues after the volunteers fly home.
